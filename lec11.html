About codehelp

CodeHelp is an online platform founded in 2020 by Love Babbar, based in Delhi, India, that offers IT-related courses such as data structures and algorithms, full-stack web development, and low-level design. It provides a range of educational resources including video courses, live doubt sessions, and a free automated teaching assistant called CodeHelp, which uses large language models with guardrails to guide students without giving direct code solutions. The platform has gained significant traction, with over 300,000 students enrolled, and offers both self-paced and live instructor-led programs. CodeHelp also provides tools for instructors to integrate the platform into their courses and monitor student progress.
